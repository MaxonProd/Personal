## Teaching philosophy

My teaching philosophy centers on practical, accessible, and outcomes-based education. I believe learning should extend beyond understanding concepts to developing the confidence and ability to apply them in real-world situations. Drawing on my professional experience in business, management, and human resources, I strive to connect academic theory with the challenges students will encounter in their careers.

I approach teaching as both an educator and a mentor, emphasizing clear expectations, constructive feedback, and individualized support. I particularly value working with adult and nontraditional learners, recognizing that students bring different experiences, responsibilities, and perspectives to their education. My goal is to maintain meaningful academic standards while creating an environment where students feel comfortable asking questions, developing their skills, and progressing at their own pace. Ultimately, I measure educational success not simply by course completion, but by a student's ability to apply what they have learned beyond the classroom.
