*I've Worked Here Before* examines how incentives, governance structures, and organizational culture shape outcomes in high-pressure environments.

The book introduces practical frameworks for considering leadership decision-making, organizational behavior, and system-level analysis. It is written as a teaching tool for business professionals, human-resources practitioners, and graduate students.

## About the work

The case study connects lived organizational experience with broader questions about accountability, throughput, incentives, and leadership maturity. Its central concern is practical: how do the systems surrounding people influence the choices they make?

## Intended readers

- Business and human-resources professionals
- Graduate and business students
- Educators working with organizational case studies
- Readers interested in leadership systems and governance
