*Common Prayer at Home* brings together selected devotional material from the 1979 *Book of Common Prayer* for personal and family use. Original introductions and explanatory notes help readers establish a regular practice of prayer and Scripture reading.

## Inside the book

- Introduction, practical reader’s guide, and further reading
- Daily Devotions for Individuals and Families
- An Order for Compline
- Prayers and Thanksgivings
- Contemporary seasonal collects
- Two-year Daily Office Lectionary
- An Outline of the Faith (the Episcopal Catechism)

The Daily Office Lectionary lists Scripture references. Keep a Bible nearby for the full readings.

## About this edition

Timothy Maxon compiled and introduced the collection and supplied original explanatory notes for selections from the public-domain 1979 *Book of Common Prayer*. This independent edition carries no official authorization or endorsement from The Episcopal Church.
