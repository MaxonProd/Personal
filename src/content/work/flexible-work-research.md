## Research overview

This qualitative multiple-case study examines remote, hybrid, and compressed four-day work arrangements in multigenerational teams. These arrangements raise practical questions about team coordination, workplace relationships, and how people across career stages are supported. The research considers those questions without presenting preliminary analysis as established findings.

## Research questions and focus

The study considers how flexible work arrangements are experienced across multigenerational teams and how team members understand their relationship to productivity, workplace relationships, professional development and mentoring, well-being, and organizational knowledge transfer.

## Methodology

The research uses a qualitative multiple-case study design. The design includes interviews, within-case analysis, and cross-case thematic analysis.

## Practical relevance

The research may inform organizational decisions about flexible-work practices, team management, employee development, well-being, and knowledge transfer. Any practical implications remain subject to the completed analysis.

## Research status

Ongoing. Passed the preliminary dissertation defense in May 2026; the dissertation remains in progress. This overview does not report study results or conclusions.
