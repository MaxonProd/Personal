## Starting condition

I acquired the rifle as a C-grade antique through Royal Tiger Imports. The brass barrel bands and butt plate were broken, and the stock had cracks and splits that affected its integrity.

## Restoration

I refinished the metal using techniques intended to maintain its original patina. I repaired the broken brass barrel bands and butt plate with silver solder and addressed the cracks and splits in the stock. I also lapped the barrel and took measurements to assess its condition against the applicable specifications.

## Ammunition

I developed functional ammunition for the rifle. The hands-on work required attention to safety and historical accuracy, and gave me another way to bring history back to life.

## Reflections

The combination of historical research, restoration, and developing historically accurate ammunition made history feel tangible to me. It was rewarding to work directly with the materials and details of an old mechanical object while keeping its history in view.
